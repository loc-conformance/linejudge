1280-matlab_block_markers_sharing_their_line

MATLAB's %{ and %} open and close a block comment only when they are alone on their line, spaces aside. The %} followed by words inside the block closes nothing, and the %{ followed by words is an ordinary % comment, so the line under it is code. A counter that takes either symbol anywhere on its line closes the block early and opens one over the last line, and the two mistakes do not cancel
