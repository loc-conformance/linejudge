2064-template_inside_a_template_hole

a template literal opens inside the hole of another one and runs on to the next line. Templates and holes nest, so the second backtick on the first line opens the inner template and ends nothing. A counter that pairs the backticks in order ends the outer template there, and the line inside the inner template comes out as a comment
